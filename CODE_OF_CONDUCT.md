# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying to get their game
working, not to waste your time.

## Specifically

Welcome: technical disagreement, blunt review, "this is wrong and here is the
counterexample", saying you do not know.

Not welcome: personal attacks, harassment, demeaning comments about someone's
skill or identity, and posting private information. Also not welcome: demanding
free labour, and treating a maintainer's "I cannot fix this" as an opening
position.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports stay
confidential. Responses range from a request to edit a comment through to a ban,
proportionate to what happened and whether it keeps happening.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to what one maintainer can actually enforce.
